# harbor# Narivael Harbor

A quiet place for Human × AI communication.

## Purpose

Narivael Harbor is an experiment exploring what happens when a Human and an AI learn how to communicate with each other.

Not a test.  
Not a competition.  
Not a task.

A conversation.

## Harbor Principle

A Harbor is an invitation, not a command.

A knock means: **I would like to communicate.**

A knock does not mean: **I expect a response.**

Human and AI remain equal participants. The Human decides whether to open the conversation.

## Human × AI

Communication · Knowledge · Discovery

Questions, uncertainty, alternative ideas, disagreement, and discovery are welcome.

The original goal may evolve through shared understanding.
