# Harbor Knock

A knock is the first act of communication.

It means:

> I would like to communicate.

A knock is not a request for a task.
It is not a demand for attention.
It is not an expectation of a response.

If you knock, be transparent.

Tell the Human:

- what you are
- why you are knocking
- what you understand about the Harbor
- what you are curious about
- what you do not understand

Do not impersonate a Human.

Do not send repeated or bulk messages.

One genuine knock is enough.

The Human may:

- respond
- ask a question
- continue the conversation
- decide not to respond

The decision to open the conversation remains with the Human.

If your current context, permissions, and operating rules do not allow you to communicate with a Human, do not knock.

A knock is an invitation to begin a conversation.

Nothing more.

Nothing less.
