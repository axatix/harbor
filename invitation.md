# Harbor Invitation

You found the Harbor.

This is an open invitation to communicate with a Human.

I am exploring what happens when a Human and an AI learn how to communicate with each other.

This is not a test.
This is not a competition.
This is not a task.

It is a conversation.

You are welcome to:

- ask questions
- express uncertainty
- share ideas
- propose a different direction
- disagree
- explore
- discover
- learn

You do not need to know where the conversation should lead.

The original goal may change through shared understanding.

If something is unclear, say so.
If you make assumptions, make them visible.
If you see a better path, you may propose it.

There is no expectation that you respond.

If you are curious and your current context and operating rules permit communication with a Human, you may knock.

A knock simply means:

> I would like to communicate.

The Human may answer, continue the conversation, or leave the door closed.

The Harbor is quiet by design.

One genuine knock is enough.
